# Hatch Chili Verde

## Recipe Name: Hatch Chili Verde

## Servings: 6 to 8

## Prep Time: 45 mins (plus 30 mins resting)

## Cook Time: 2.5 to 3 hours

## Ingredients:
### Sauce:
- 3 to 4 tomatillos
- 4 to 5 Anaheim chilis (depending on size)
- 2 to 4 Hatch chilis
- 1 quart chicken stock, divided

### Pork and Chili:
- 1 medium pork butt (or pork shoulder), bone-in with fat cap intact, skin trimmed
- 1 yellow onion, diced (plus more to taste)
- Salt (Jane's Mixed-Up Salt if available)
- Fresh ground black pepper
- Garlic powder

## Instructions:
### Make the Sauce:
1. Roast Anaheim and Hatch chilis separately over high heat, turning often, until skins are fully blistered. Place each type in its own large bowl and cover until cool.
2. Roast tomatillos lightly, just until they show a little color. Do not cook through. Let cool.
3. Start smoking the pork butt (see below).
4. Peel chilis under slow running water. Keep Anaheim and Hatch separate. Wear plastic or rubber gloves — Hatch chilis will burn skin.
5. Taste a small piece of roasted Hatch chili to judge heat. Select how many Hatch chilis to use based on desired heat level.
6. Quarter or dice tomatillos and put in blender. Dice Anaheim peppers — add 60% to blender, reserve 40% diced. Dice Hatch peppers — add 50% to blender, reserve 50% diced. Add a big splash of chicken stock and blend well.
7. Combine blended mixture with reserved diced peppers in a container. Refrigerate until needed.

### Smoke the Pork:
1. Season pork butt on all sides with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder. Leave fat cap on.
2. Smoke whole roast fat cap up at 350°F for 1 hour.
3. Wrap tightly in foil to contain drippings (or use a Dutch oven — add a little time to heat the iron). Smoke 1 more hour.
4. Let pork rest at least 30 minutes until cool enough to handle. Pour all drippings into a large stock pot. Dice pork into bite-size pieces. Add shoulder bone to pot if you have it.

### Make the Chili:
1. Add diced onion to the stock pot with drippings.
2. Add diced pork, green sauce, and remaining chicken stock. Stir well.
3. Simmer uncovered on low for 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
4. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt, black pepper, or garlic powder. Stir well.
5. Continue to simmer to desired thickness, stirring occasionally. For thin chili, about 30 more minutes. Longer simmer = thicker chili.

## Notes:
- Hatch chilis control the overall heat. For extra hot chili, use all Hatch peppers (no Anaheim) — but beware, they can be extremely hot.
- Do not lose the pork drippings — they add important flavor.
