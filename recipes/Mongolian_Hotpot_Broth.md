1. In a large pot, combine water and kombu. Bring to a simmer over medium heat.
2. Add the ginger and garlic, allowing to simmer for 10 minutes to infuse the flavors.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook for an additional 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the soy sauce, miso paste, and any additional seasonings to taste.
5. Remove from heat before adding the hot pot ingredients of your choice (meat, vegetables, noodles).
6. Serve with dipping sauces on the side.
